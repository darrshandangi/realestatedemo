Who We Are

Defining Luxury,
One Space at a Time

Atelier Noir Interiors is a New York-based design house specializing in ultra-luxury residential, commercial, and hospitality environments. For over fifteen years, our team of world-class designers has transformed the most discerning visions into immersive spatial experiences.

We don't follow trends — we set them. Every project begins with deep listening: understanding not just what a space should look like, but how it should feel, breathe, and evolve alongside its inhabitants.

"True luxury is invisible — it's the feeling of walking into a room and knowing everything is exactly where it belongs."

— Isabelle Marchand, Founder
